# User Persona: Alex Thompson

## Basic Information

- **Name**: Alex Thompson
- **Age**: 32
- **Occupation**: Software Engineer
- **Location**: Urban area, works remotely
- **Tech Proficiency**: High
- **Financial Goals**: Budgeting, debt reduction, and financial independence

## Background

Alex is a tech-savvy professional who values organization and data-driven decisions. They're comfortable with technology and prefers using digital tools to manage various aspects of their life, including personal finance. Alex is looking for a solution that provides clear insights into their spending habits and helps them achieve their financial goals.

## Pain Points

1. **Fragmented Financial Picture**: Using multiple apps for budgeting, expense tracking, and investment management
2. **Time-Consuming**: Manual entry of transactions across different platforms
3. **Lack of Customization**: Existing tools don't allow for personalized budget categories
4. **No Clear Visibility**: Hard to track progress toward financial goals
5. **Security Concerns**: Wary of cloud-based financial tools

## Needs & Goals

- **Visibility**: Clear visibility into their financial situation and future financial plans
- **Automation**: Automatic categorization of transactions
- **Customization**: Ability to create and modify budget categories
- **Insights**: Visual representation of income vs. expenses
- **Security**: Self-hosted option for better control over financial data
- **Cross-Device Access**: Optimized for mobile and desktop access to financial information
